Your Personal finance tracker

A personal finance tracker is a powerful tool designed to help you monitor, manage, and optimize your financial life by centralizing all your money habits in one place. By systematically logging your income, daily expenses, investments, and debts, it provides a clear, real-time snapshot of your overall net worth and cash flow. Instead of guessing where your hard-earned money goes each month, a tracker categorizes your spending into clear buckets like housing, groceries, and entertainment.
